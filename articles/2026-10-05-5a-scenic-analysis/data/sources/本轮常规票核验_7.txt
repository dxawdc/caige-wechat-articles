The giant statue of Guanyin(Avalokitesvara) stands 108 meters tall on an artificial island overlooking the South China Sea from Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone, in Sanya, Hainan province. [Photo/chinadaily.com.cn]

Located just 40 kilometers southwest of downtown Sanya, Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone has been known as the most blissful Buddhist land. “Good fortune as vast as the East Sea, long life as great as Nanshan Mountain” is a famous saying that depicts Nanshan as the root of Chinese traditional culture emphasizing auspiciousness and longevity.

With an area of 34.7 square kilometers, the tourism zone is divided into three theme parks: Nanshan Buddhism Culture Park is a window into China’s traditional Buddhist culture; Felicity and Longevity Culture Park reflects an atmosphere of peace, harmony and longevity; and Nanshan Cultural Park highlights China’s diverse social customs.

Image

Nanshan Temple is a large building complex that imitates the architectural style of the Tang Dynasty (618-907). Its main structures are Renwang Hall, Bell Tower and Golden Hall (the main hall), covering 5,500 square meters. [Photo/nanshan.com]

More than just beautiful scenery, Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone uniquely blends tropical seaside landscapes with rich Buddhist culture, folk customs and historic legends. Visitors can enjoy spiritual comfort and a harmonious connection with nature amid the sun, sea, sand and lush greenery.

The Nanshan vegetarian cuisine is a must-try, embodying the essence of traditional Buddhist vegetarian practices. Its ingredients include wild mushrooms, bean products, konjac fiber and locally sourced seasonal vegetables and fruits.

## 南山文化旅游区

南山文化旅游区是中国南部重要的佛教文化园区，以108米海上观音、南山寺与园林景观闻名。这里三面环海，文化底蕴深厚，是祈福、静心与欣赏海天风光的必到景点。

## 108 米海上观音的震撼景象

海上观音立于南海之上，是世界最高的观音造像之一。三尊面向不同方向，象征祝福与守护。阳光照射下圣像泛起温暖金光，与海天相接形成壮丽景观。游客可步行栈道抵达近距离观赏，是景区最具震撼力的核心点位。

## 南山寺与佛教文化建筑群

园区拥有南山寺、三十三观音堂与多处佛教主题建筑，呈现典型江南园林与南海海岸线融合的风格。殿宇之间香烟缭绕，钟声回荡，使人感受到沉静力量。文化展示区介绍南海观音文化与海南佛教发展脉络，极具参观价值。

## 园林步道与静心漫游体验

园区设计大量步道、花园与水景区域，可在树荫下漫步、聆听海风。清晨与傍晚的光线最柔和，适合拍照与散步。三面环海的地势带来开阔视野，是放松心情、体验慢生活的最佳场所。

## 素斋文化与心灵疗愈氛围

景区内提供清淡健康的南山素斋，以本地蔬果为主，口感自然舒适。游客常将午餐与祈福结合，体验佛系度假的完整旅程。整体氛围宁静，是“想放空”游客的治愈系景区。
